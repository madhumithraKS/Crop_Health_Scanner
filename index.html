Project Overview & Problem Statement

Challenge: Unpredictable climatic changes, sudden pest outbreaks, and nutrient imbalances lead to heavy crop loss and agricultural disasters.

Our Solution: An integrated IoT & AI-driven early warning risk monitoring framework designed to protect yields before hazards escalate.

Key Features

  • Real-time IoT Sensor Monitoring (Soil pH & Moisture).
  • AI-powered Leaf Disease & Deficiency Scanner.
  • Automated Early Warning Hazard Risk Alerts.
  • Integrated Simulation & Hardware Telemetry (ESP32/OLED).
