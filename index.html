SAEAWRX

Smart Early Crop Health Detection and Risk Monitoring System

1. Problem Statement

Farmers face heavy crop losses due to late detection of plant diseases and unpredictable environmental risks. Traditional methods lack real-time monitoring and early warning capabilities.

2. Proposed Solution

SAEAWRX provides an AI-driven, automated early scanning system that monitors crop health continuously, detects abnormalities instantly, and predicts risks to protect crop yields.

3. Key Features & Workflow

  • Real-time AI Scanning: Fast image and data analysis for early disease identification.
  • Risk Monitoring Dashboard: Live status alerts and risk indicators for farmers.
  • Cloud Deployment: Accessible anywhere instantly via high-speed secure links.
